MUSIC REFERENCE SYSTEM

SCALEVOX

OFFICIAL SUPPORT

SCALEVOX is a reference app for musicians who need quick access to scales, music theory, and tuning information. It helps you check the notes you need while composing, performing, practicing, or producing music.

HOW TO USE

1

Choose a Key

Select the root note you want to use as your reference.

2

Select a Scale

Browse major, minor, modes, pentatonic scales, and world scales.

3

Check Notes

View the scale tones as text or on a piano keyboard.

4

Use Theory

Reference key signatures, intervals, the circle of fifths, and more.

5

Check Tunings

Look up tunings for guitar, bass, ukulele, strings, and world instruments.

6

Use It Anywhere

Keep it close during practice, writing, recording, and live work.

FEATURES

SCALE REFERENCE

Quickly check notes for essential scales and more advanced scale types.

PIANO VIEW

See scale tones visually on a keyboard for fast, intuitive reference.

THEORY GUIDE

Reference pitch, key signatures, intervals, chord-scale ideas, and the circle of fifths.

TUNING GUIDE

Find tunings for guitar, bass, ukulele, classical strings, folk instruments, and more.

FAQ

Do I need to sign in?

No. SCALEVOX can be used without login or account registration.

Does the app record audio?

No. SCALEVOX does not record, analyze, or upload audio.

Can I use it offline?

The app's core scale, theory, and tuning references are available inside the app.

What if I find incorrect information?

Please contact us with the scale name, tuning item, or theory entry in question.
